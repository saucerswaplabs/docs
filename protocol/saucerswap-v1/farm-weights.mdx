V1 farms receive 1,200 of Masterchef's 5,000 allocation points during Phase 1: 24% of the base SAUCE emission rate. Individual farm points are integers and sum to the V1 allocation. Governance can change both the top-level allocation and the weights of individual farms.

## Calculate a farm's share

A farm's share of the V1 budget is its allocation points divided by 1,200. Its share of all point-allocated Masterchef rewards is its allocation points divided by 5,000:

$$
R_f = 1.056992 \times \frac{p_f}{5{,}000}
$$

Here, $R_f$ is the farm reward rate in SAUCE/sec, and $p_f$ is the farm's allocation points. The V1 allocation therefore receives 0.25367808 SAUCE/sec in total. Your reward depends on your share of the LP tokens staked in that farm. The additive devcut is separate from these rewards; see [SAUCE tokenomics](/tokenomics/overview#masterchef-emissions).

## Current farm weights

Use the [active farms API](/api-reference/rest/farms/list-farms) for each farm's SAUCE emission rate and the [web app](https://www.saucerswap.finance/) for current rewards and APR. Read allocation points from each farm's configuration in [Masterchef on HashScan](https://hashscan.io/mainnet/contract/0.0.1077627). The API reports the resulting per-farm rate in whole SAUCE/min; divide by 60 to compare it with the SAUCE/sec formula above. It does not expose allocation points.

Adjusting a farm's points changes its share of the reward budget. Check all active V1 farm points together rather than carrying forward percentages from an earlier allocation. The other Phase 1 top-level destinations are V2 LARI at 3,500 points and the DAO at 300 points.
