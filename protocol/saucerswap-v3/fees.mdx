---
title: "V3 fees and rebates"
description: "Understand SaucerSwap V3's approved market fees, maker rebate caps, xSAUCE discounts, and revenue routing, with calibration changes pending activation."
---

V3 fees apply to executed fills. Takers pay for removing liquidity, while eligible makers can receive rebates for supplying it. Fees are configured per market and can differ by account; always review the fee shown for your order before signing. Order-book execution has no separate interface fee; wallet setup transactions can still incur network fees.

<Info>
  As of September 8, 2026, the market schedule below is approved under the [V3 calibration proposal](https://gov.saucerswap.finance/t/saucerswap-v3-order-book-calibration-contract-migration-market-set-and-fee-configuration/385) but has not activated yet. It replaces the original launch schedule when the recalibrated markets open. Until then, use the app's effective fees rather than these planned rates.
</Info>

## Approved market fee schedule

The maker column shows a negative fee: SAUCE/HBAR's −0.05% means a rebate of up to 0.05% of the filled value, before the safety cap. One basis point (bp) equals 0.01%.

| Market | Taker fee | Maximum maker rebate before discounts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SAUCE/HBAR | 0.20% (20 bps) | −0.05% (−5 bps) |
| DOVU/HBAR | 0.80% (80 bps) | −0.20% (−20 bps) |
| WETH/HBAR | 0.10% (10 bps) | −0.025% (−2.5 bps) |
| SAUCE/USDC | 0.25% (25 bps) | −0.0625% (−6.25 bps) |
| HBAR/USDC | 0.12% (12 bps) | −0.03% (−3 bps) |

These are the calibration's starting parameters, not a promise that every account receives the maximum rebate. Minimum order sizes and the approved scope for later parameter adjustments are covered in [V3 markets](/protocol/saucerswap-v3/markets).

## Maker rebate safety cap

For the recalibrated markets, the applied maker rebate cannot exceed 25% of the effective taker fees collected on the fill, after xSAUCE discounts. This prevents rebates from exceeding their funding source. A quoted maker rebate can therefore be reduced when the counterparty's taker discount lowers the fee collected.

<Accordion title="Example: how a taker discount changes the rebate cap">
  Assume a SAUCE/HBAR fill with a value equivalent to $1,000. At the planned 20 bps taker fee, an account with a 20% xSAUCE discount pays 16 bps, or $1.60.

  The maker rebate is capped at 25% of $1.60, or $0.40. Although the market's listed maximum rebate is 5 bps ($0.50 on this fill), the applied rebate cannot exceed $0.40. The example isolates fee allocation and does not model settlement costs or token-price changes.
</Accordion>

Maker rebates are paid from trading fees, not from [Masterchef emissions](/tokenomics/overview#masterchef-emissions) or [Liquidity-Aligned Reward Initiative (LARI)](/protocol/saucerswap-v2#liquidity-aligned-reward-initiative-lari) rewards. A resting order earns no rebate merely for being present on the book; it must receive an eligible maker fill.

## xSAUCE fee discounts

Holding xSAUCE gives your account a tiered discount on the market's baseline fee schedule. The [V3 launch proposal](https://gov.saucerswap.finance/t/saucerswap-v3-launch-economics/368) established these thresholds, and the calibration leaves them unchanged:

| Minimum xSAUCE balance | Discount |
| --- | --- |
| Below 10,000 | 0% |
| 10,000 | 5% |
| 50,000 | 10% |
| 250,000 | 15% |
| 1,000,000 | 20% |
| 5,000,000 | 30% |
| 10,000,000 | 40% |

A discount reduces a positive taker or maker fee. If the maker fee is negative, the tier can increase the rebate, but the rebate safety cap still applies; holding more xSAUCE does not guarantee a larger payment on every fill. To acquire xSAUCE through staking, see [Single-sided staking](/protocol/single-sided-staking).

## Check the effective fee

| Surface | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Trade page order preview | The fee quoted for your order before signing |
| `GET /books` | Public market configuration, including baseline maker and taker fee fields |
| `GET /fees/:orderbookId?side=maker` or `side=taker` | Account-specific fee configuration for a market; wallet authentication required |

The API expresses fees in pips: 1 pip = 0.0001%, and 100 pips = 1 bp. The `capFractionPips` field expresses the rebate cap as a fraction of effective taker fees; it is not a separate trading fee. See the [Orderbook API reference](/api-reference/orderbook/market-data#fee-units) for the units and authentication requirements.

<Note>
  [Market makers](/resources/market-makers), API users, and enterprise participants may have separate written fee or rebate programs under the [terms of service](/legal/terms-of-service). Public market parameters do not establish an account's eligibility for those programs.
</Note>

## Where net fees go

After applicable trader discounts and maker rebates, V3 net fees fund SAUCE buybacks through Unified BrewSaucer. Purchased SAUCE is allocated 40% to Mothership/xSAUCE, 30% to the Development Fund, 10% to burn, and 20% to the protocol-owned liquidity (POL) + Incentive Reserve.

This is the Phase 1 allocation from the later [SAUCE alignment proposal](https://gov.saucerswap.finance/t/hbar-usd-perpetuals-sauce-emission-reduction-and-unified-brewsaucer/396). It replaces the older source-specific V3 split independently of the pending V3 market calibration. See [SAUCE tokenomics](/tokenomics/overview#unified-brewsaucer-buybacks-and-burn) for the complete revenue flow and the separate devcut burn.
