SaucerSwap charges an interface fee on swaps made in its own apps.

| App | Interface fee |
|---|---|
| SaucerSwap web app | 0.40% |
| SaucerSwap mobile app | 0.50% |

## How it works

- The fee is charged on the swap output.
- It is calculated on the output before the fee is taken. A swap with an output of 1,000 units before the fee pays 4 units on web.
- It is paid in addition to the pool fee. The pool fee is set by each pool, for example 0.15% or 0.30%, and is not part of the interface fee.
- Swaps between two of these stablecoins pay a lower interface fee, 0.05% on web and on mobile: USDC, USDC[hts], USDT[hts], DAI[hts] and USDT0. Any other pair pays the rate in the table.
- Two kinds of swap pay no interface fee: swaps whose output token charges its own fee on transfer, and swaps from a short list of exempt accounts.
- Other apps that route swaps through SaucerSwap's pools may add their own fees. Those are set by those apps, not by SaucerSwap.

## Stock trades

Stock trades on the web app pay a separate SaucerSwap fee of 0.25%. It is taken from the input amount and paid on Sonic, not to the account below. Stock trading is not in the mobile app.
