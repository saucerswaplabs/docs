Each two-week Liquidity-Aligned Reward Initiative (LARI) epoch distributes rewards across V2 pools according to weights set by [governance](/governance/overview). During Phase 1, V2 LARI receives 3,500 of Masterchef's 5,000 allocation points, or 70% of the base emission rate. See [SAUCE tokenomics](/tokenomics/overview#phase-1-allocation) for the protocol-wide allocation.

The dated epoch budget below is the published distribution allocation. It is separate from the continuous Masterchef funding rate: 44.393664 SAUCE/min, equivalent to approximately 894,976.27 SAUCE over a full 14 days at an unchanged rate. Use the published epoch budget for per-pool distributions rather than recalculating it from an assumed activation time.

### HBAR rewards
