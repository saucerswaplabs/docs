## "Is this token or project a scam?"

We cannot answer that for any one token. SaucerSwap does not review tokens, and a token appearing in the app is not an endorsement or a check on its reserves, backing or value (see our [terms](/legal/terms-of-service#12-token-pair-pool-orderbook-market-route-and-stablecoin-availability)).

- Anyone can list a token. Listing is permissionless.
- Tokens outside the default list ask you to accept a disclaimer first. Read it.
- Check the token yourself on [HashScan](https://hashscan.io/mainnet/dashboard): the token ID, who made it, and what the token's own site says.
- Treat a look-alike symbol as a different token until its ID matches.

## "Are you scamming me?"

You are right to ask. Here is how to check us.

- SaucerSwap is non-custodial. Labs does not store your keys or hold your assets.
- Contract IDs are public: [deployed contracts](/developers/contracts).
- Audit reports are public: [audits](/developers/security/audits). An audit covers only what it reviewed. It is not a promise that nothing can go wrong.
- Check every on-chain action on [HashScan](https://hashscan.io/mainnet/dashboard).
